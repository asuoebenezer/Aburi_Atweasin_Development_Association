About Us

Rooted in community.
Driven by nature.

Aburi Atweasin Development Association is an environmental non-governmental organisation committed to protecting our natural world and the communities that depend on it.

From tackling climate change and plastic pollution to restoring forests, safeguarding biodiversity and training young people to be environmentally conscious, we believe lasting change begins with informed, empowered people.

  • Community-led environmental action
  • Practical climate adaptation and mitigation
  • Educating and mobilising the next generation

Plastic Pollution

Turning the tide on plastic

Plastic bottles and bags choke our gutters, rivers and seas, harm wildlife and break down into microplastics that enter our food chain. We work with communities, schools and businesses to reduce, reuse and recycle.

Re-afforestation

Growing forests, growing hope

Trees cool our towns, protect soil and water, store carbon and shelter wildlife. We plant native species with communities and follow up to make sure every seedling has the chance to become a forest.

  • Community tree-planting campaigns
  • Native tree nurseries and seedling care
  • Restoring degraded and bare land

Youth Training

Raising environmentally conscious youth

Young people will inherit the consequences of today's choices, so they must lead the solutions. Our training programmes build knowledge, skills and confidence to protect the environment at home, at school and in the community.
